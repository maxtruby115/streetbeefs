SPECIAL & ULTIMATE

Your meter holds two charges. SP uses one charge for your special. ULT (or ↓ + SP) needs both charges and uses them all. Fill the meter by landing hits, blocking and taking damage.

Death Sentence SPDEATH GRIP: leaps onto the opponent's back and pounds them for 2 seconds, then kicks off and knocks them down. Dodge it by moving, jumping or hitting him mid-leap. Mash buttons to shake him off sooner
Death Sentence ULTSUPER SAIYAN: points at you, "Prepare to be slaughtered like the pig you are." Then purple flames for 8 seconds. Invincible for the first 3, 50% more damage and 25% faster
White Woof SPHOWL: a sonic blast from his voice that hits 6 times and knocks the opponent down
White Woof ULTFULL MOON: turns into a hulking white werewolf for 8 seconds. Bigger reach, claws, invincible for the first 3 seconds, 50% more damage and 30% faster
Big Mac SPOLLIE SHUFFLE: about 3 seconds of super speed with afterimages, plus 20% more damage
Pappas SPELEMENT OF SURPRISE: teleports behind the opponent in a puff of smoke and throws a heavy punch that knocks them down
Pappas ULTFOURTH WALL: jumps at the camera, gets huge and punches the screen. Cracks the glass and does big damage. Can't be blocked
Vic SP (DLC)OOF: a wound-up punch that sends the opponent flying across the map
Vic ULT (DLC)ANDERDINGUS: becomes a floating shadow for 8s; punch and kick shoot black fireballs
Green Thumb SP (DLC)SHOVEL: throws a fast spinning shovel straight ahead, jump it to dodge
Green Thumb ULT (DLC)ROOTED: a tree grows under the opponent and its roots trap and squeeze them
Max SP (DLC)GOLF CART: rides a super fast golf cart across the whole map
Max ULT (DLC)WILD CARD: a random ultimate from another fighter
Cherry Bomb SP"FOR ALL THE SINGLE MOMS OUT THERE" then a huge wind-up slap that sends them flying
Cherry Bomb ULTCHERRY BOMB: lobs a massive red bomb that lands on the opponent and explodes for huge damage. Can't be blocked. Get out of the blast
Mr Dangerous SP"LETS DO THIS" then a traveling spinning kick that hits up to 4 times and knocks down
Mr Dangerous ULT"I AM DANGEROUS" then rushes in, puts the opponent on the ground and kicks them like crazy for 2 seconds. Mash to escape sooner. Jump to dodge the rush
Big Mac ULTEASY FIGHT: leaps up (can't be hit) and ground slams. The shockwave hits anyone standing on the ground nearby for huge damage and can't be blocked. Jump to dodge it

GRAB (G)

Press GRAB right next to the opponent. Grabs beat blocking. While holding them press P or K for your finisher, or it happens on its own. Just got grabbed? Press GRAB right away to break free.

MOVEMENT & DEFENSE

→ →Dash (double-tap)
← ←Backdash, briefly invincible
Hold ←Block high and mid. Hold ↙ to block lows
COUNTERTap toward your opponent right as their attack is about to hit. You take no damage, they're stunned, and you get a free hit. Doesn't work on ultimates or grabs
CombosJab, crouch jab, roundhouse and uppercut can cancel into your special when they connect

KEYS

P1: W A S D move · J punch · K kick · L special · I ultimate · O grab · P taunt. Against the CPU the arrows with Z X C V B N also work.

P2: arrows move · , punch · . kick · / special · ' ultimate · ; grab · [ taunt.

Touch: put your thumb anywhere on the left half for the stick. BLK holds block. T taunts: it builds meter, but you can be hit while you show off.
